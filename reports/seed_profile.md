## What this means for the build

- The seed names issues, symptoms and a generic fix. It holds no causes, so causes, diagnostics and runbooks come from vendor documentation and hand-written gold procedures.
- Severity and resolution time are dropped: they change between copies of the same issue.
- Accuracy is reported per canonical issue, never per row, and held-out tickets are split by canonical issue.
